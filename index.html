Playing as

按住人物可以拖着走。学校黑板 = 闯关；卧室：上床睡觉、开关灯、玩具箱；客厅：沙发、电视、厕所门。
跳床游戏：你控制的角色在床上跳，点屏幕跳下去，家人在床边接住后会举起来转一圈。
Bed jump: your character bounces on the bed. Tap the screen to jump, and a family member catches you and spins you around.
厨房：冰箱拿冰淇淋、巧克力马上吃；水果要先洗再吃；蔬菜要洗 → 煮 → 吃。
Kitchen: ice cream and chocolate from the fridge can be eaten right away. Fruit must be washed first. Vegetables: wash → cook → eat.
Hold a character to drag it. School blackboard = quiz. Bedroom: beds, lamp, toy box. Living room: sofa, TV, toilet door.
